# Lifted programs: what the compiler's choice adds

Median of the runs (min-max). `generic`: the program's own queue class lifted onto
the general-purpose queue (wtc-cc --no-specialize); `compiler`: lifted, with the
compiler's choice; `port`: the hand port onto wtc::queue, through wtc-cc.
compiler/generic above 1 is the compiler's gain.
